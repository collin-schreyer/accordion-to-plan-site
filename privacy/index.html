Accordion to Plan

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

Schreyer Data & Automation publishes Accordion to Plan, a VR rhythm game for Meta Quest. This policy explains the game’s local data, development recordings, and information you send us for support.

What the game saves

Your scores and grades, selected song and difficulty, instrument settings, and hand or controller calibration are saved on your headset. They let the game remember your progress and preferences. Local settings and custom-song records are not uploaded to the developer. The game also supports optional Meta-hosted world leaderboards for the built-in songs, as described below.

World leaderboards

When world score sharing is enabled, completed standard push/pull performances of built-in songs submit their song, difficulty, and ranked score to Meta’s leaderboard service under your Meta account. Other players can see your public Meta name, rank, and score. The game uses app-scoped user IDs to identify your entry and display nearby rankings. IDs and profile names are held in memory for that session; we do not send them to a separate developer-operated server or use them for advertising.

You can turn world score sharing off in Settings and continue playing and keeping scores locally. Turning sharing off or uninstalling the game does not remove scores already stored by Meta. Contact us to request removal of an existing leaderboard entry. Leaderboard availability also depends on Meta’s platform approval and connectivity.

Your own MIDI songs

You may select a MIDI through the headset’s system file picker. Only that selected file is read. After you confirm that you wrote the music or have permission to use the MIDI, the file, selected melody track, song title, and permission confirmation are saved in the game’s private app-data folder. Custom songs and their scores remain local; the game does not upload the files, share them with other players, or place custom charts on the public leaderboards.

Headset, hand, and controller tracking

The game uses headset position and orientation, hand poses, or controller inputs supplied by Meta Quest to place the accordion and respond to your playing. It does not access raw camera images or record your microphone.

Development and test recordings

Development and test builds can include a local performance recorder used for testing and trailer production. When enabled, it starts a take when the app opens or resumes and closes the take when the app pauses or quits.

A take contains headset and hand or controller poses, finger poses, input states, song timing, gameplay events, scores, the date and time, device model, and app and engine versions. It also records the game’s own audio output. These takes are saved in the game’s private app-data folder on the headset. The recorder retains the newest 12 takes and removes older ones as new takes begin.

Recordings are not automatically uploaded or shared. A developer or tester can manually copy their recordings from the headset for testing or trailer production. This feature is intended for development builds; the data behavior of the public release will be reflected in this policy before release.

Platform and engine services

Meta handles your Meta account, store activity, and platform services under the Meta Privacy Policy. For leaderboards, the game requests your app-scoped Meta user ID and public profile name. It does not request your friends list or payment details and does not display or retain profile photos.

The game is built with Unity. The project disables Unity Analytics, cloud diagnostics, advertising, and purchasing services, and does not include a developer-operated analytics or advertising service. Unity and Meta may handle technical information through their engine and platform services under their own policies. See Unity’s game player privacy notice.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or sensitive information. Support messages are kept only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up.

Your choices and deletion requests

Clear the game’s app data or uninstall it through your headset’s settings to remove its local saves and development recordings. To ask about or request deletion of information you have sent us through support, email collin.schreyer@gmail.com. Requests concerning your Meta account or store transactions should be directed to Meta.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or limits on the use of your personal information. Contact us at the address below to make a request.

This website

These pages do not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or third-party forms. Fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting provider may process connection information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the pages. If hosted on GitHub Pages, that processing is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when the game’s data behavior changes.

Which headsets and inputs are supported?

Accordion to Plan is designed for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. You can play the whole game with bare hands or use Touch controllers. Pick up the controllers to switch inputs, or put them down to return to hands.

Can I sit down?

Yes. The game supports sitting or standing. Use the Quest system recenter if the stage is in the wrong place. The game aligns the performer’s position when it receives a tracked headset pose.

The bellows or keys feel out of reach

Open Settings and choose Recalibrate. The game keeps separate bellows calibrations for hands and controllers. During calibration, choose a comfortable closed and open position.

How do I pause?

With hands, turn your left palm up and pinch. With controllers, press the menu button. Opening the Quest system menu also pauses the song.

How do I remove my saved data?

Clear the game’s app data or uninstall it through your headset’s settings. This removes local scores, grades, settings, calibrations, and any development recordings. Read the privacy policy for more information, or email us about information you have sent through support.
